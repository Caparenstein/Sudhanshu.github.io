My Robotics Hardware & Systems

Every system below is a physical robot I designed, modeled, wired, and calibrated. I focus on combining mechanical design with mathematical control theory — utilizing classical PID, modern ADRC with Extended State Observers, Fuzzy Logic, and LQR optimal control.

MY KINEMATIC SCHEMATIC // 5-DOF MANIPULATOR PID • ADRC • FUZZY

My 5-DOF Robotic Arm & Precision Gripper

I engineered this articulated mechanical manipulator with 5 full degrees of freedom: Base Yaw (θ1), Shoulder Pitch (θ2), Elbow Pitch (θ3), Wrist Pitch (θ4), Wrist Roll/Twist (θ5), and an active parallel grabbing mechanism. I programmed a hybrid control architecture combining classical PID for fast tracking, ADRC (Active Disturbance Rejection Control) to estimate and cancel joint friction and varying payload masses using an Extended State Observer (ESO), and Fuzzy Logic for adaptive gain scheduling.

5-DOF Kinematic Chain Parallel Gripper Jaws ADRC Observer (ESO) Fuzzy Gain Scheduler PID Servo Tracking
MY GIMBAL SCHEMATIC // 2-AXIS VISION TURRET PID CLOSED-LOOP

My Active Face-Tracking Camera Turret

I built this high-speed dual-axis Pan-Tilt gimbal system powered by precision metal-gear servos to track targets in real-time. I wrote an active computer-vision pipeline that calculates pixel coordinate errors of detected faces and cursor targets, feeding cross-axis errors into a custom discrete PID controller. By tuning anti-windup clamping and derivative damping, I eliminated servo oscillation and achieved fluid, organic tracking.

Pan-Tilt Gimbal OpenCV Face Detection Discrete PID Control Anti-Windup Clamping Zero-Overshoot Damping
MY TOPOLOGY SCHEMATIC // 4-ROBOT COLLABORATIVE SWARM CONSENSUS • APF

My Swarm Robotics: 4 Autonomous Collaborative Units

I developed this decentralized multi-agent system consisting of exactly 4 autonomous mobile robots working in unison. I implemented leader-follower consensus protocols and Artificial Potential Fields (APF): virtual repulsive forces prevent inter-robot collisions while attractive forces guide the robots toward exploration goals. When commanded, all 4 robots immediately compute collision-free vectors and converge to a common coordinate.

4 Swarm Agents Decentralized Consensus Artificial Potential Fields Collision Avoidance Live Click Convergence
MY DYNAMICS SCHEMATIC // WHEELED-LEGGED LQR BALANCE LQR OPTIMAL: u = -Kx

My Variable-Height Self-Balancing Bot (LQR)

I engineered this transformable two-wheeled inverted pendulum robot with articulated servo knee linkages. It can crouch low (35%), maintain mid stance (65%), or extend tall (100%) while preserving active upright dynamic balance. To stabilize the bot as the leg linkages shift the center of mass, I implemented LQR (Linear Quadratic Regulator) Control Theory, solving the Continuous Algebraic Riccati Equation to continuously recalculate optimal state-feedback gain matrix u = -Kx.
